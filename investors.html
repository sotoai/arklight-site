The Problem

Industrial Mobilization Gap

200:1 shipbuilding. 15,000+ aircraft backlogged. 7-year submarine delays.

The AI and robotics revolution is outpacing the workforce. Production gaps widen across every critical defense and manufacturing subsector while the talent pipeline was designed for a world that no longer exists. The U.S. industrial base cannot mobilize at the speed its national security demands.

Talent Pipeline Collapse

2M unfilled roles today. 3.8M projected by 2033. 50% retirement-eligible.

Structural talent collapse across manufacturing, defense, and advanced technology. Half the current manufacturing workforce will retire within the decade. Traditional education produces credentials, not capability — graduates with degrees but without the skills to operate in AI-native, high-stakes environments.

Broken Hiring Signal

$1.7T in education debt. $52B CHIPS. $369B IRA — stalled by the wrong workforce.

The hiring system relies on proxies — degrees, resumes, interviews — that cannot predict performance in rapidly evolving environments. Billions in federal capital sit idle because the workforce to deploy it does not exist. The system charges for credentials but doesn't deliver operators.

The Opportunity

Convergence

AI, robotics, and automation are transforming every industry simultaneously. The workforce must evolve at the same speed — or American industrial leadership stalls. Every sector is competing for the same talent that doesn't exist yet.

The Vacuum

Next-generation defense and industrial companies are scaling rapidly but cannot find enough qualified talent through traditional pipelines. No existing player owns the full stack: assessment, training, and deployment.

Capital Availability

$52B (CHIPS) & $369B (IRA) neutralized by a workforce that doesn't exist yet. Significant non-dilutive capital through DoD, DoL, and state workforce programs awaits the company that can deploy talent at scale.

Timing

Demand, capital, policy, and technology are converging. The company that builds the workforce infrastructure for this era captures a generational opportunity. The window is now — before incumbents adapt or the gap becomes irreversible.

The Solution

ARC Assessment

AI-native talent evaluation that replaces resumes and interviews with quantified performance telemetry — aptitude, learning velocity, and mission readiness measured under pressure. A 19-construct battery built on military-grade psychological research, adapted for the AI era. Currently onboarding design partners across defense and advanced manufacturing.

Talent Factories

Manufacturing facilities that will execute real government and commercial contracts while training the next-generation workforce in advanced manufacturing, AI-native operations, and emerging IT. Production and education become one system — workers train on live contracts, not in classrooms.

The Interlock — Closed Loop

ARC identifies talent → Talent Factories develop them on real work → performance data flows back to ARC → assessment accuracy compounds. Each cycle tightens the prediction-to-performance loop, creating a proprietary data flywheel no competitor can replicate.

Moat — Three Layers

  • Physical layer: Manufacturing facilities executing real production contracts, generating revenue and training environments
  • Data layer: Proprietary assessment corpus and continuous performance telemetry — every hire validates and refines ARC
  • Talent layer: Workers assessed, trained, and deployed through Arklight's system — creating switching costs and network effects

Competitive Landscape

No existing player combines AI-native assessment, production-integrated training, and manufacturing facility ownership. The landscape is fragmented across six categories, each addressing only a sliver of the problem.

Staffing Firms

Fill seats, don't build talent. Resume-based, high churn, no assessment depth. Cannot predict performance in AI-native environments.

Trade Schools

Classroom-first, no production revenue, slow to update curricula. Graduates arrive with certifications but not operational readiness.

Bootcamps

Software-focused, no manufacturing or defense relevance. No physical infrastructure, no contract execution capability.

Assessment Vendors

Generic psychometrics not built for high-stakes industrial roles. No closed-loop validation, no training layer, no production data.

DoD Programs

Bureaucratic, slow to scale, not commercially viable. Solve for compliance, not capability. Cannot attract non-traditional talent.

Large Primes

Build internal training but don't export it. Captive talent pools, no incentive to solve the industry-wide pipeline problem.

White space: Arklight is the only company building a vertically integrated assess-train-deploy system with production revenue, proprietary talent data, and physical facility ownership — purpose-built for the AI era of defense and manufacturing.

Product Deep Dive: ARC Assessment

The Problem ARC Solves

The current hiring system relies on proxies — degrees, resumes, interviews — that cannot predict performance in rapidly evolving, high-stakes environments. Companies in defense and advanced manufacturing need a fundamentally different signal: one that measures how someone thinks, learns, and performs under pressure.

Architecture: 19 Constructs Across 3 Domains

ARC evaluates candidates through 19 scientifically grounded constructs organized into three domains:

Cognitive Core (6 constructs)

  • Adaptive Problem Solving
  • Cognitive Processing Speed
  • Working Memory Under Load
  • Learning Velocity
  • Decision Quality Under Pressure
  • Error Recovery & Adaptation

Technical Aptitude (8 constructs)

  • Systems Reasoning & Diagnostics
  • Pattern Recognition & Anomaly Detection
  • Technical Math & Quant Reasoning
  • Digital Logic & Computational Thinking
  • Spatial & Process Visualization
  • Attention Stability Under Load
  • Fault Isolation & Debug Speed
  • Information Security Instinct

Behavioral Integrity (5 constructs)

  • Reliability & Rules Compliance
  • Values Conflict Scenarios
  • Behavioral Consistency Cross-Check
  • Mission Alignment Indicator
  • Integrity Flag Detection

Role Cutlines

Each role has a defined cutline — a minimum construct profile required for success. Cutlines are not arbitrary thresholds; they are empirically derived from the performance patterns of top operators in each role. ARC doesn't just rank candidates — it predicts fit for specific positions.

Differentiation

  • Purpose-built for industrial roles: Not adapted from generic HR psychometrics — designed from the ground up for defense, manufacturing, and high-stakes operations
  • AI-native: Adaptive testing, real-time scoring, dynamic difficulty adjustment based on candidate performance
  • Quantified, not qualitative: Every candidate receives a numerical profile across 19 constructs, enabling data-driven hiring at scale

Closed-Loop Advantage

As Talent Factories come online, real production data will continuously validate and refine assessment accuracy. Every worker trained and deployed generates performance telemetry that feeds back into ARC — closing the gap between prediction and performance. This creates a compounding data advantage that grows with every assessment and every hire.

Product Deep Dive: Talent Factory

I. Acquire & Modernize

Arklight will acquire manufacturing facilities — many from retiring owners without succession plans — and install AI-native operating systems, modern equipment, and digital-first workflows.

These facilities will execute real government and commercial contracts, generating revenue while serving as training environments.

II. Train on Real Work

Production and education will become one system. Workers will train on live production — not in classrooms — across three tracks:

  • Advanced manufacturing: CNC, precision machining, quality systems
  • AI-native operations: AI-augmented workflows, automation, data-driven decision making
  • Emerging IT: Cybersecurity, networking, and operational technology

III. Deploy to Partners

Validated workers — assessed by ARC, trained on real contracts — will deploy to design partners across defense, aerospace, and advanced manufacturing.

The closed loop:

  • ARC identifies elite talent from non-traditional backgrounds
  • Talent Factories develop them through real production
  • Partners receive workers who are proven, not just credentialed

IV. Facility Acquisition Strategy

Target: small-to-mid manufacturing facilities ($2M–$15M enterprise value) with retiring owners and no succession plan. The U.S. has tens of thousands of these facilities — a generational transfer of manufacturing capacity is underway.

  • Defense-adjacent subsectors and geographies prioritized
  • Existing contracts and customer relationships transfer with acquisition
  • AI-native modernization increases throughput and margin post-acquisition

V. Contract Sourcing Strategy

Talent Factories will be de-risked by demand before acquisition. Contract sourcing strategy includes:

  • Subcontracting through existing prime relationships
  • Direct government contracts (GSA, DoD, state procurement)
  • Commercial manufacturing contracts from design partners
  • Workforce development contracts (DoL, state workforce agencies)
